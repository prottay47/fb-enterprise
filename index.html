About F B Group

Bridging UAE's quarries with Bangladesh's growth.

F B Group was founded to solve a simple problem: Bangladesh's construction boom needs hard, durable stone, and the UAE's Hajar mountains produce some of the strongest gabbro and limestone aggregates on earth.

From quarry face to vessel, we manage the full chain — extraction, crushing, screening, port logistics and bulk shipping — so contractors in Dhaka, Chittagong and Khulna receive consistent, on-spec material on schedule.

Quarry-Direct Sourcing

Owned access to UAE quarry production, no middleman markup.

Bulk Shipping

Geared and gearless vessels from Fujairah and Khor Fakkan.

Spec Consistency

Every shipment tested for size, shape and impact value.

End-to-End Logistics

Documentation, customs, port handling — all managed by us.

Our Strength

Complete Control. Uninterrupted Supply. Delivery to Every Destination.

We manage the entire supply chain — from premium bulk stone sourcing and international shipping to vessel discharge, customs clearance, stockyard management and final delivery to project sites.

With our own vessels, self-discharging systems, cranes, trucks, equipment and logistics operations, we minimize third-party dependencies, reduce unloading delays and maintain complete control over quality, timing and supply commitments.

    01

    Lighter Vessels & Barges

    A fleet of 60+ owned lighter vessels and barges ensures efficient mother-vessel discharge and uninterrupted transportation of bulk aggregates to our stockyards and destinations.

    Our dedicated self-discharging facilities and equipment enable faster, more efficient bulk cargo unloading, minimizing vessel turnaround time and operational delays.

  3. ⛓️
    03

    Conveyor Unloading System

    Continuous-flow conveyor systems facilitate efficient, controlled and reliable cargo discharge directly from vessels to designated stockpiles.

  4. 🚚
    04

    Dump Truck Fleet

    Our fleet of 50+ owned dump trucks supports reliable transportation and direct delivery of bulk stone and aggregate from stockyards to project sites across Bangladesh.

  5. 🏗️
