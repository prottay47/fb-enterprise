About F B Group

Connecting global quarries with Bangladesh's growth.

F B Group supplies the stone and sand that keep Bangladesh's construction moving. We source premium stone and construction aggregates from the UAE, Oman, India, China, Thailand and Bhutan, and supply locally sourced sand from across Bangladesh.

From international bulk imports and vessel discharge to customs clearance, stockyard management and project-site delivery, we run the full supply chain with our own vessels, trucks and teams — so contractors across the country receive consistent material, on schedule.

Multi-Country Sourcing

Premium stone and aggregates from the UAE, Oman, India, China, Thailand and Bhutan.

Local Sand Supply

Construction sand from across Bangladesh for concrete, plaster and filling.

Bulk Shipping & Discharge

Owned and chartered lighter vessels with self-discharging capability for faster turnaround.

Direct-to-Site Delivery

Our own dump trucks deliver from our stockyards to project sites nationwide.

Our Logistics Advantage

Five key capabilities. One seamless supply chain.

From mother-vessel discharge to final project-site delivery, our dedicated logistics resources ensure efficient cargo handling, faster turnaround and dependable bulk transportation.

🚢

Available Lighter Vessels

Our dedicated fleet of owned and chartered lighter vessels and barges ensures cargo movement from mother vessels to stockyards and designated destinations. Our vessel availability supports continuous operations and minimizes waiting time.

🏗️

Self-Discharging Capability

Our self-discharging capabilities enable efficient bulk cargo unloading directly from vessels, reducing dependence on external unloading arrangements and helping minimize discharge delays.

🛳️

Bulkhead Vessels

Dedicated bulkhead vessel capacity supports the transportation and handling of bulk stone and aggregate cargo, ensuring dependable movement between mother vessels, stockyards and delivery points.

⚙️

Advanced Cargo Handling Capacity

Equipped with modern E-Cranes and heavy-duty material handling equipment, we ensure efficient, reliable and seamless cargo operations with a handling capacity of up to 10K MT per day, delivering speed, precision and operational excellence to meet large-scale cargo handling demands.

🚚

Dump Truck Fleet

With our own dump truck fleet, we provide reliable last-mile transportation of bulk stone chips and aggregates from our stockyards directly to project sites across Bangladesh.

Bangladesh Yards

Spacious bulk cargo unloading & stockpiling yards in Chittagong and Dhaka.

Material is held at our own dumping yards near Chittagong port and on the Dhaka river belt, so it reaches your project site fast, regardless of where you build.

Our Strength

Complete Control Uninterrupted Supply Delivery to Every Destination

We manage the entire supply chain — from premium bulk stone sourcing and international shipping to vessel discharge, customs clearance, stockyard management and final delivery to project sites.

With our own vessels, self-discharging systems, cranes, trucks, equipment and logistics operations, we minimize third-party dependencies, reduce unloading delays and maintain complete control over quality, timing and supply commitments.
