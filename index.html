About FB Enterprise

Bridging UAE's quarries with Bangladesh's growth.

FB Enterprise was founded to solve a simple problem: Bangladesh's construction boom needs hard, durable stone, and the UAE's Hajar mountains produce some of the strongest gabbro and limestone aggregates on earth.

From quarry face to vessel, we manage the full chain — extraction, crushing, screening, port logistics and bulk shipping — so contractors in Dhaka, Chittagong and Khulna receive consistent, on-spec material on schedule.
